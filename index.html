Schengen visa: document checklist

A Schengen visa is a short stay visa, up to 90 days, covering 26 European countries including France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands and Switzerland. These are the documents usually required:

  • Valid passportMust be valid beyond your planned travel dates.
  • CNICYour national identity card, valid and clear.
  • FRC (Family Registration Certificate)Issued by NADRA, shows your family record.
  • MRC, if marriedMarriage Registration Certificate, also from NADRA.
  • Bank statement with account maintenance certificateShows your financial position; the maintenance certificate confirms the account is genuinely yours.
  • NTN and 3 years tax returns, if in businessProof of a declared, running business.
  • 6 months pay slips and job letter, if employedProof of stable employment.

Checklist last reviewed: 15 Oct 2026. Requirements can change, so confirm your list with us on WhatsApp before you apply.
